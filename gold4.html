HOW WE WORK

Pick or design

Choose from our floor collection, or bring a reference photo — our karigars can rework it in a fitting.

Weighed and priced, transparently

Gold weight, stone weight, and making charges are shown separately, against that day's published rate.

Hallmark verified at counter

We show you the hallmark and, on request, verify it live on the BIS Care app before billing.

Certificate, invoice, and after-care

You leave with a purity certificate, GST bill, and a standing offer of free cleaning and polishing.
